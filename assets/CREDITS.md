This document describes the origins, adaptations, and licensing of visual assets used in the EvoluPark patient journey animations.

**License:** Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal  
**License URL:** https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

### Usage in EvoluPark

Humaaans illustrations are used to represent patients, healthcare professionals, and other characters in educational animations explaining the EvoluPark clinical study.

The original vector illustrations have been adapted using Figma, including:

- Custom character compositions and poses.
- Modified skin, hair, and clothing colors.
- Walking animation sequences.
- SVG exports for integration into Python/Manim animations.
